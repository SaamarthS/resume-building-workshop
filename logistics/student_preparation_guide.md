# Student Pre-Workshop Preparation Guide

> **Distribution:** Send to registered attendees 48 hours before the workshop via email / announcement channels.

---

## Welcome to the Resume Building Workshop!

In this 75-minute hands-on session, you will build a professional, publication-quality technical resume using **Overleaf (LaTeX)** and sharpen your project bullet points using **Google AI Studio**.

To make sure you leave the session with a completed, exported resume PDF, please complete these 3 quick setup steps **before arriving**:

---

## 1. Required Accounts (Complete in Advance)

1. **Overleaf Account:**
   * Go to [https://www.overleaf.com/register](https://www.overleaf.com/register) and create a free account (using your university email or personal Google account).
   * Verify your email address so you can immediately copy templates.
2. **Google Account (for Google AI Studio):**
   * Visit [https://aistudio.google.com/](https://aistudio.google.com/) and ensure you can log in with your Google account.
   * Accept the standard terms of service so you are ready to enter prompts immediately.

---

## 2. What to Bring to the Workshop

* [ ] **Your Laptop & Charger:** You will be actively typing and compiling code during the session. (Tablets and phones are *not* suitable for LaTeX editing).
* [ ] **3–4 Rough Projects in Mind:**
  * You do **not** need polished descriptions!
  * Simply note down:
    - What the project was called.
    - What technologies/languages you used (e.g. Python, React, C++, Arduino).
    - What features you personally built.
    - Any real result or link (GitHub repo, live deployment, or course lab).

---

## 3. Important Ground Rules

* **We do not invent achievements:** If your project was a classroom assignment or hobby prototype, that is completely fine! We will teach you how to describe your real engineering work accurately, rather than making up fake metrics.
* **No LaTeX experience needed:** We will provide a clean template and teach you the exact commands needed to customize it.
